# Codetrail 1.1.0

A licensing release: Codetrail is now under the Apache License 2.0. Nothing changes in how Codetrail reads your code or writes your guide.

## Changed

- **Codetrail is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/mohamed-abdelsattar92/codetrail/blob/v1.1.0/LICENSE)** instead of the GNU AGPL 3.0 only. You may use, change and share it, commercially or not, inside a closed product or as a service, without asking. Keep the copyright and licence notices and say which files you changed. Copies of 1.0.0 and 1.0.1 keep the AGPL. The decision is recorded in [ADR 0012](https://github.com/mohamed-abdelsattar92/codetrail/blob/v1.1.0/docs/adr/0012-apache-2-license.md).

## Removed

- **The contributor licence agreement.** Contributions are licensed under the Apache License 2.0, as its section 5 says, with nothing to sign; the pull request template asks you to confirm you have the right to submit the work. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/mohamed-abdelsattar92/codetrail/blob/v1.1.0/CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **The commercial licence offer**, which the Apache License makes unnecessary.

The name "Codetrail" and its logo are still reserved: the licence grants no rights to them (section 6), and [TRADEMARKS.md](https://github.com/mohamed-abdelsattar92/codetrail/blob/v1.1.0/TRADEMARKS.md) says what you may do with them.

## Added

- **A Sponsor button** on the GitHub repository, linking to Ko-fi.

## Install or upgrade

```bash
uv tool install --reinstall git+https://github.com/mohamed-abdelsattar92/codetrail@v1.1.0
```

```bash
codetrail --version
```

Nothing to migrate: your targets, guides and settings carry over as they are. The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/mohamed-abdelsattar92/codetrail/blob/v1.1.0/CHANGELOG.md).
